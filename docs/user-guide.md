# User guide

One section per area of the system. What you can see depends on your role; if a menu item or button is
missing, you do not have that permission (ask your Owner). Amounts are shown in your business currency.

## Home

Shows today's picture: sales today, this week and this month, orders waiting, overdue follow-ups, low
stock and balances owed. Each figure appears only if you may see it. Click a figure to see the records
behind it. The bell shows notifications (a new conversation to take, a task due, low stock).

## Products

_Products_ lists what you sell. Open a product to change its name, price, category, brand, images and
**variants** (the sellable versions, e.g. size or fabric, each with its own SKU and price). A product is
**stockable** (counted in inventory), **non-stockable** (a service) or **made to order**. Furniture
attributes such as material and colour are filled in on the product. Archive instead of deleting;
archived products stay on old orders. Categories and brands are managed under _Products → Categories /
Brands_.

## Customers

Add customers with one or more phone numbers; the system warns about duplicates and lets you merge
them. A customer's page shows orders, payments, balance, notes, conversations and a timeline of
everything that happened.

## Leads and tasks

A **lead** is a possible sale. Use the board (columns are stages) or the list. A lead from WhatsApp
appears by itself. Move it through the stages, assign it to a salesperson, add notes, and mark it
**lost** with a reason. **Tasks** are follow-ups with a due date.
**Convert** a lead to a customer and an order when the sale is real.

## Quotations

Create a quotation from a lead or customer. Add catalogue lines or **custom lines** (for example a
sofa made to size; fill in the measurements). _Send_ it, then _Accept_ when the customer agrees
(by phone, message or in person) and _Convert to order_. A sent quotation is a fixed snapshot: later
price changes do not alter it. Download or send the PDF. Unaccepted quotations expire on their date.

## Orders

An order moves through **draft → confirmed → deposit paid → in production → ready → out for delivery →
delivered → completed** (or cancelled). Rules to know:

- Production cannot start until the required deposit (50 % for furniture by default) is paid.
- An order cannot be completed while a balance is owed unless you have the permission to override.
- Discounts above your limit, or price changes, need a manager.
- The order page has the payments, the delivery details, the documents (confirmation PDF and invoice)
  and a history of every change.

## Payments

Record a **deposit** or **order payment** on the order page or from _Payments_: choose the method
(cash, bank transfer ...) and amount. Payments needing checking (e.g. a bank transfer) wait for
**confirmation** by someone with that permission. A wrong payment is **voided** with a reason; it stays
on record. A customer who pays more than the balance, or pays in advance, has **credit** that can be
applied to a later order. Each payment gets a numbered receipt. _Receivables_ lists who owes what and
how overdue it is.

## Expenses and accounts

_Expenses_ records money going out (rent, transport ...) against an account. _Accounts_ are your cash
box, bank accounts and wallets; their balances follow the payments and expenses. Accounts ticked _show
to customers_ supply the bank details sent to customers.

## Inventory

Shows stock on hand, reserved for orders, and available, per product variant. **Opening stock** sets the
starting quantity. **Adjust** corrects it and always needs a reason. Every change is a line in the
stock history that cannot be edited. Variants under their minimum are flagged **low stock**. Confirmed
orders **reserve** stock; delivery deducts it.

## Point of sale

For walk-in customers. Search or scan products, set quantities, give a discount within your limit,
choose the payment method and **Charge**. Stock is deducted, the sale is recorded and a **receipt**
prints in 58 mm, 80 mm or A4. _Reprint_ marks the copy as a reprint. If stock is not enough the sale is
refused and nothing is recorded. Tapping Charge twice is safe; it is one sale.

## Purchasing

_Suppliers_ holds your suppliers. Create a **purchase order**, send it, and **receive** goods (partly or
fully); receiving adds stock and updates the average cost. _Quick purchase_ records something bought
and received at once.

## Commissions

Each salesperson has a percentage. When an order is completed (or a counter sale is made) a
commission is created. _Commissions_ lists them; a manager **approves** and accounts **pays** them.
Cancelling an order reverses its commission. _Performance_ shows sales per person. Salespeople see only
their own.

## Conversations (WhatsApp)

All WhatsApp chats in one inbox. A new chat creates a lead. Reply from the box below the chat. Tick
marks show sent, delivered and read. You can attach a quotation or order PDF, use a **quick reply**, or
send a **template** (e.g. the bank-details message fills in the customer's name, order number, total
and your bank accounts). Outside 24 hours after the customer's last message only approved templates can
be sent. Assign a chat to a colleague; unread counts are shown. The AI panel (below) sits beside the
chat.

## AI assistant

When switched on by the Owner, the panel beside a chat shows a **summary**, the **details it found**
(each marked high, medium or low confidence, and whether it is really in the chat), **matching
products**, a list of **what is still missing** with the next question to ask, and a **draft reply**.
Nothing happens until you press **Approve**: approving the details fills the lead; approving the draft
sends it (you can edit it first). Drafts that promise prices, availability or discounts are flagged for a
person to check. You can switch the AI off for a single chat.

## Reports

Sixteen reports (sales by date, by product, by staff, payment methods, receivables, stock, low stock,
commissions and more). Pick the dates, click a row to see the records behind it, and **Export CSV**.
Financial reports need the financial permission. Exports stop at 5,000 rows.

## Search and notifications

The search box (press **/**) finds customers, leads, orders, products and quotations by name, number or
phone. The bell lists notifications; click one to go to the record and mark it read.

## Your account

Use the account menu to change your password and sign out.
